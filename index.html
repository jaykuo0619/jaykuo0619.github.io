01 · Process spec

One person, two process flows.

I'm a Production Control Integration Planner in TSMC's Manufacturing Planning Integration Division, after two years on 3D Fabric advanced-packaging planning, with dual master's degrees in Computer Science (Georgia Tech) and Information Management (University of Washington). Fabs run on two things: a plan the business can trust, and the data plumbing that keeps that plan honest. I do both.

As an engineer, I build the automation (VBA tools that query SQL directly, multi-database pipelines, self-serve HTML reports) that removes hours of manual work from every week. As a planner, I own the Fab Daily Indices issued every morning and the Fab Daily Detection Report, turning that plumbing into decisions on lots, pull-ins and wafer starts.

My TSMC story started as an intern during a warehouse-automation rollout, and grew into building the tools the planning floor runs on.
